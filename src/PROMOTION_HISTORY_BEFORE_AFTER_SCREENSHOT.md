## 💡 **KEY UNDERSTANDING**

### **Why NOT "2026/2027 → 2026/2027"?**

This makes NO SENSE because:
- ❌ Students can't be promoted FROM and TO the same session
- ❌ That would mean they didn't move at all
- ❌ Confusing for historical records

### **Why "2025/2026 → 2026/2027"?**

This makes PERFECT SENSE because:
- ✅ Shows the transition: FROM current year TO next year
- ✅ Clear historical record of when promotion happened
- ✅ Matches actual academic calendar

### **Why Just "2025/2026" for Graduation?**

This makes PERFECT SENSE because:
- ✅ Students graduate IN a specific year
- ✅ They're not moving TO another session
- ✅ Clear record of which year they completed studies
